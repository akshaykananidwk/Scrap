# User guide

## Getting started

1. **Register** at `/register` with your mobile number and business details. It is free.
2. **Verify your mobile** — we send a code. You cannot list or bid until you do.
3. **Complete your business profile** — name, type, GSTIN, PAN, address, contact.
4. **Complete KYC** — upload your GST certificate, PAN, registration and address proof.
   Verified businesses get more enquiries and can join restricted auctions.

One account can both buy and sell. Change it any time under Dashboard → Profile.

## Selling

### Post a listing
Dashboard → **Sell Scrap** opens a nine-step wizard:

1. Title, sale method and category
2. Material and grade (and HSN code for the invoice)
3. Quantity and unit — with your minimum order quantity
4. Price and GST — or leave the price blank for "price on request"
5. Condition and source
6. Location, pickup address and who can collect
7. Photos, videos and documents — listings with photos get far more enquiries
8. Trade terms: payment terms, who loads, who transports, inspection
9. Review and publish

If listing approval is switched on, your listing is reviewed before it appears. Verified sellers are
approved automatically.

### Run an auction
Choose **Auction** as the sale method, then set it up on the listing's Auction tab:

- Forward (buyers bid up) or reverse (suppliers bid down)
- Starting price, bid increment and an optional hidden reserve
- Start and end time (shown in IST, stored in UTC)
- Anti-sniping: extend by N seconds if a bid lands in the last N seconds, up to a maximum
- Who can bid: KYC-verified only, approved by you, deposit required, maximum bidders
- Whether bidder names are masked publicly

The auction console shows live bids, registered bidders, the event log, and lets you approve or
block bidders and award the auction when it ends.

### Handle offers
Buyers send offers on negotiable listings. Accept, reject, or counter — every round is recorded, so
nothing rests on a phone call. Accepting creates the order.

## Buying

### Find material
**Buy Scrap** filters by category, material, grade, quantity, price, condition and location. Save a
search you run often.

### Post what you need
**Post a requirement** and every seller who deals in that material is notified. For a multi-item
purchase, raise an **RFQ**: add your line items, invite suppliers (or leave it public), and compare
quotes side by side before awarding.

### Bid in auctions
Open a live auction, register if required, and place your bid. Bids must clear the current price by
at least the increment. If you are outbid you are notified immediately. A bid placed in the final
moments extends the auction, so sniping does not work here.

## Completing a deal

Every deal becomes an **order** that both sides can see.

1. **Confirm** the order
2. **Arrange transport** — assign a vehicle, driver, LR number and e-way bill
3. **Weigh the material** — record the weighbridge slip: gross, tare and net weight, plus any
   deduction with a reason
4. **Accept the weighment** — the other party confirms, and the order re-prices on the actual weight
5. **Pay** — record UPI, NEFT, RTGS, cheque or cash with the UTR; the seller confirms receipt
6. **Invoice** — the seller raises the GST invoice; print it or save it as PDF from the browser
7. **Complete and review** — rate the other party

### Why weighment matters
Scrap is sold on an estimate and settled on fact. Your final amount is calculated from the weight
the weighbridge actually recorded, not the quantity in the listing. Both sides see the same slip.

## If something goes wrong

Raise a **dispute** from the order page. Pick a category, describe what happened, attach evidence
and state the amount you are claiming. Our team sees the full deal history — listing, messages,
weighment, payments — and works with both sides to settle it.

Keep the deal on the platform: off-platform payments are not covered by dispute resolution.
